THE STUFF WE REFUSED TO PUT UP FRONT.

these are deliberately spoiler-heavy notes from the finished crossover. lock the mode again whenever you want the clean version.

01 / THE DEVICE

THE TRANSDIMENSIONALISER

Turquoise's Transdimensionaliser 2000 causes the worlds to collide. Later, the remaining shots become a serious problem as the group tries to get everyone home.

02 / THE LIGHT

THE BUTTON WAS A TERRIBLE IDEA.

The group reaches the strange light and, naturally, somebody presses a button. The light disappears, leaving everyone dealing with something much bigger than they expected.

03 / RED

THE THREAT IS VERY REAL.

Red becomes the central danger. The different groups eventually cooperate against him, including a particularly important assist from Nerdbot.

04 / CYAN

NOT EVERYONE MAKES IT HOME FIRST TRY.

After the apparent victory, Cyan is missing. The others have to go back through the dimensional mess to find him.

05 / EXQ & TURQUOISE

THE CROSSOVER ACTUALLY MATTERS TO THEM.

EXQ Genius and Turquoise form an unexpectedly meaningful friendship. The ending gives EXQ a way to visit again instead of treating the crossover like a one-off.

06 / EPILOGUE

THE MOVIE ENDS. THE PROBLEM DOESN'T.

The returning characters make it home — only for the Bot Games ending to immediately hint that their problems are not over.
